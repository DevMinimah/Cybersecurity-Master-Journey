# Module 02: Incident Response, BC & DR (Domain 2)

## 📅 Date Started: 2026-04-23
## 📅 Date Completed: 2026-04-23

## 🎯 What I Learned:
- Business Continuity (BC): Purpose, key components, and organizational resilience planning
- Disaster Recovery (DR): Recovery strategies, RTO/RPO concepts, and restoration procedures
- Incident Response Lifecycle: Preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned
- Coordination Protocols: Roles, communication channels, and escalation procedures during incidents

## 💡 Key Takeaways:
- BC and DR are complementary but distinct: BC keeps operations running; DR restores after disruption
- A structured incident response process minimizes damage and accelerates recovery
- Documentation and post-incident reviews are critical for continuous improvement
