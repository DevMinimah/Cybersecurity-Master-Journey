## 📅 Date Started: 2026-04-022
## 📅 Date Completed: 2026-04-022

## 🎯 What I Learned:
- CIA Triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability as core security principles
- Authentication & MFA: Methods for verifying identity and strengthening access security
- Risk Management: Risk identification, assessment, tolerance, and treatment strategies
- Security Controls: Technical, administrative, and physical control types and applications
- ISC2 Code of Ethics: Professional conduct requirements for cybersecurity practitioners
- Governance Frameworks: Policies, procedures, standards, and regulatory compliance fundamentals

## 💡 Key Takeaways:
- The CIA Triad is the universal foundation for evaluating security decisions
- Risk management is proactive—not reactive—and requires continuous assessment
- Security controls must be layered and aligned with organizational risk tolerance
- Ethical conduct is mandatory, not optional, in the cybersecurity profession
