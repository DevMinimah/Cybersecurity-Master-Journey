## 🎯 What I Learned:
- Cybersecurity profession: Roles, responsibilities, and career pathways in the field
- Historical context: Significant events that shaped the development of cybersecurity
- Organizational impact: Importance of cybersecurity to business operations and risk management
- Entry-level competencies: Foundational skills and knowledge required for cybersecurity roles
- Professional frameworks: Industry standards and best practices for security professionals
- Security mindset: Developing analytical thinking for identifying and assessing security risks

## 💡 Key Takeaways:
- Cybersecurity is essential for protecting organizational assets, data, and operations in the digital age
- The field has evolved in response to significant historical events and emerging threats
- Entry-level roles require a combination of technical knowledge, analytical skills, and continuous learning
- Security professionals play a critical role in maintaining business continuity and protecting sensitive information
- Understanding the threat landscape is fundamental to effective security practice
