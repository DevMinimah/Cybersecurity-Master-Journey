# Course 01: Practical Assignment - Identify Social Engineering Through Phishing

## 📅 Date Started: 2026-05-27
## 📅 Date Completed: 2026-05-27

## 🎯 What I Learned:
- Phishing email analysis: Systematic approach to examining suspicious emails for technical and behavioral indicators
- Technical indicators: Identifying definitive red flags such as mismatched sender domains, suspicious URLs, malformed headers, and unexpected attachments
- Prioritization skills: Distinguishing between minor red flags and the most suspicious, definitive technical indicator of a phishing attempt
- URL analysis: Checking for domain spoofing, misspellings, suspicious subdomains, and HTTP vs HTTPS protocols
- Header inspection: Understanding email header fields to verify sender authenticity and trace email routing
- Attachment risks: Recognizing dangerous file types and unexpected attachments as potential malware delivery vectors
- Decision framework: Applying a structured methodology to evaluate multiple red flags and select the most critical indicator

## 💡 Key Takeaways:
- Not all red flags are equal, focus on definitive technical indicators over subjective or contextual cues
- The most suspicious indicator is often technical: mismatched domains, suspicious links, or forged headers not just poor grammar or urgency
- Phishing detection requires both technical knowledge and critical thinking to avoid false positives
- A single definitive technical indicator can confirm a phishing attempt even if other elements appear legitimat
- Many at times urgency is one of the quickest way to identy phishing attempts
- Practice builds pattern recognition and repeated exposure to phishing scenarios improves speed and accuracy in real-world scenarios
