## 🎯 Challenge
Lead the end-to-end design, implementation, and management of a cybersecurity infrastructure for a professional services organization recovering from a significant malware and insider threat incident. Transition the organization from a reactive security posture to a proactive, resilient, and compliance-aligned operational state.

---

## 📋 Incident Context (Redacted)
- Organization Profile: Professional services firm with strict data confidentiality and regulatory requirements
- Incident Background: Prior breach involving malware propagation and internal threat activity
- Objective: Rebuild security architecture, establish continuous monitoring, implement risk controls, and ensure operational resilience
- Scope: Network/cloud strategy, SOC design, threat mitigation, data protection, and recovery planning

---

## 🌐 Phase 1: Infrastructure & Cloud Strategy
- Objective: Assess existing architecture and establish a secure foundation for data and applications
- Network Reconnaissance: Mapped network topology and software assets to identify vulnerabilities and establish security monitoring baselines
- Cloud Deployment Model: Recommended and implemented a Private Cloud architecture
- Rationale: High-compliance environment required absolute control over sensitive data, isolation capabilities, and customized security controls that public/hybrid models could not guarantee
- Outcome: Secure, compliant infrastructure foundation aligned with business continuity requirements

---

## 🛡️ Phase 2: Security Operations Center (SOC) Design & Implementation
- Objective: Establish dedicated capabilities for continuous monitoring, threat detection, and incident response
- SOC Model Selection: Evaluated Virtual, Hybrid, SOCaaS, and In-house models against operational requirements
- Selected Model: In-House SOC
- Strategic Justification: Maintains maximum control over security measures, enables direct oversight of private infrastructure, and ensures fastest response times without third-party latency
- Team Assembly & Role Assignment:
  - Prioritized experienced personnel for immediate impact and minimal ramp-up
  - Aligned skills to critical functions: threat hunting, incident response, security engineering
  - Integrated compliance-specific training (e.g., confidentiality protocols, privilege handling) into continuous improvement cycles

---

## 🔍 Phase 3: Comprehensive Risk Assessment & Mitigation
- Objective: Identify threats, analyze business impact, and design targeted controls
- Threat Categorization & Control Mapping:

| Threat Category | Specific Risk | Mitigation Control Implemented |
|----------------|---------------|--------------------------------|
| External | Phishing targeting personnel | Security awareness training + advanced email filtering |
| External | Physical breach of infrastructure | Enhanced facility security + access control protocols |
| Internal | Insider threat activity | Least privilege access + continuous behavioral monitoring |
| Internal | Weak credential management | Strong password policies + mandatory MFA enforcement |

- Business Impact Analysis: Prioritized investments based on potential data loss, operational disruption, and reputational damage
- Outcome: Risk-driven control implementation aligned with organizational tolerance and compliance obligations

---

## 💾 Phase 4: Data Resilience Strategy (Backup, Recovery & Encryption)
- Objective: Ensure data integrity, availability, and confidentiality during and after security incidents
- Backup Plan Development:
  - Assigned dedicated ownership to IT Lead for execution and verification
  - Scope: Full coverage of electronic organizational data
  - Methodology: Differential backups balancing resource efficiency with recovery speed
  - Storage Strategy: 3-2-1 Rule implemented (3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite)
- Recovery Plan Formulation:
  - Immediate: Restore from verified clean backups
  - Hardening: Apply patches to address exploited vulnerabilities
  - Review: Conduct structured lessons-learned sessions with stakeholders
  - Prevention: Tighten network rules and close identified gaps
- Encryption Strategy (Layered Approach):
  - Data at Rest (Endpoints): Full-disk encryption on all workstations
  - Data in Transit (External): Asymmetric encryption for sensitive external communications
  - Data in Transit (Internal): Public key encryption for internal network data sharing
  - Backup Data: Symmetric encryption for efficient large-volume protection
- Outcome: Comprehensive data resilience framework ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability across all states

## 🛡️ Security Principles Demonstrated

| Principle | Implementation | Business Impact |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Defense in Depth | Private cloud isolation + host encryption + network controls + SOC monitoring | Limits blast radius and ensures multi-layered threat containment |
| Risk-Based Security | Impact analysis driving control prioritization and investment decisions | Maximizes ROI on security spend while addressing highest-value threats |
| Data Resilience | 3-2-1 backups + differential strategy + phased recovery sequencing | Ensures operational continuity and minimizes downtime post-incident |
| Compliance Alignment | Tailored controls for confidentiality, access logging, and privilege management | Reduces regulatory exposure and maintains client trust in high-stakes industries |
