# Module 01: Data and Privacy - Theory

## 📅 Date Started: 2026-05-01
## 📅 Date Completed: 2026-05-02

## 🎯 What I Learned:
- Introduction to Cybersecurity: Why cybersecurity is important and common threat types
- Key Elements of Cybersecurity: Core principles and how to apply them in practice
- Data Privacy: Understanding data types, data protection, and privacy best practices
- CIA Triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability as foundational security principles
- Security Parameters & Controls: Key security parameters and implementation of controls
- Data Loss Prevention: Strategies to prevent data loss
- Encryption: Importance of encryption including email, network, file, and hard disk encryption
- Data Backup: Why backups are needed, responsibilities, what data to back up, and backup types
- Career Paths: Cybersecurity career paths, data privacy careers, data security analyst roles, and EC-Council Certified Encryption Specialist (ECES) program

## 💡 Key Takeaways:
- Cybersecurity protects data, systems, and people through multiple layers of defense
- The CIA Triad is the foundation for all security decisions and frameworks
- Data privacy is critical for protecting personal and organizational information
- Encryption is essential for protecting data in transit and at rest across multiple channels
- Regular backups are crucial for data recovery and business continuity
- Multiple career opportunities exist in cybersecurity specialization areas
